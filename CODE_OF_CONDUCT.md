# Code of Conduct

## A welcoming community

Everyone is welcome to contribute to the Coriolis STACKIT Installer, regardless
of background, identity, experience, or ability. This code applies to project
issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-hosted discussions, including
participation by maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and patient.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, rather than attacking people.
- Respect different perspectives and experience levels.
- Accept corrections, acknowledge mistakes, and help keep discussions productive.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information without consent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal insults,
doxxing, and retaliation against people who raise concerns are unacceptable.

## Reporting concerns without email

For content on GitHub, use the reporting options in the content's menu. If
**Report to repository admins** is available, use it to notify the project's
administrators without opening a public issue. Availability depends on the
repository settings and your permissions.

For violations of GitHub's policies, including harassment or threats, use
[GitHub's abuse reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
These reports go to GitHub Support, not to the project maintainers. This route is
also available when a concern involves a maintainer. GitHub decides how to handle
reports under its own policies.

See [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for details. Do not publish sensitive allegations, personal information, or
private evidence in an issue or pull request. The project does not currently
provide a separate private channel for conduct reports.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for applying these standards fairly and considering
the context and severity of incidents brought to their attention. They may ask
for a change in behavior, remove inappropriate content, lock a discussion, or
restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Serious incidents may warrant
immediate restrictions.

Maintainers should explain moderation decisions to the affected person when
practical, limit disclosure of sensitive information to what is needed to handle
the incident, and avoid handling cases in which they are personally involved.
Good-faith reports must not result in retaliation.
